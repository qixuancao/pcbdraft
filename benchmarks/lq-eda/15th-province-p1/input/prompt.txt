The circuit topology shown in official figure 3 is:

- U13 pin 5 PROG connects to R18; the other end of R18 connects to GND.
- U13 pin 4 VDD connects to VBUS.  C14 is from VBUS to GND.  R19 starts at
  VBUS and feeds LED4; R20 starts at VBUS and feeds LED5.
- U13 pin 1 STAT is the common LED cathode node.  LED4's anode goes through
  R19 to VBUS as `LED4_A`; LED5's anode goes through R20 to VBUS as `LED5_A`.
- U13 pin 2 VSS connects to GND.
- U13 pin 3 VBAT connects to CN1 pin 1 and to the positive side of C13;
  the other side of C13 and CN1 pin 2 connect to GND.
